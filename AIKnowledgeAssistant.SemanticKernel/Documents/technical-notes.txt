Technical Notes

Deployment
Production deployments happen every Thursday at 14:00 after the release checklist is complete.
Every deployment needs a rollback plan and a second reviewer.

Branching
Feature branches are created from main and named feature/short-description.
Pull requests need at least one approval and a passing build before merging.

Incidents
Production incidents must be reported in the incident channel within 15 minutes.
A short post-incident review is written within 2 working days.
